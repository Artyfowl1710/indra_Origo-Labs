INDRA RELEASE VALIDATION FUNDING PLAN
22 November 2026

Requested: INR 150,000, once.
Applicant: Mihir Khatri, India, individual maintainer.
Origo Labs is a project name, not a registered company.

Proposed 12-week effort, starting after funding and schedule agreement. Budget: INR
120,000 for 240 hours of maintainer work at INR 500/hour; INR 18,000 for temporary
GPU/model-server test access; INR 12,000 for access to additional Windows test
environments and packaging validation. Expenses are planning allowances, not vendor
quotes. Weeks 1-4: test the existing installer with fresh profiles on at least two
additional Windows PCs, record prerequisites and startup failures, fix reproducible
issues, and publish sanitized results. Weeks 5-8: exercise one documented llama.cpp
configuration and one Linux/WSL vLLM configuration through Indra's OpenAI-compatible
connection, record exact runtime/model/hardware versions, and add regressions for
confirmed failures. Weeks 9-12: publish repeatable timing, memory and successful-output-
throughput measurements with scripts and raw synthetic-input results; exercise the
existing signed-model launcher against a real small local bundle, including altered-file
rejection; update installation and troubleshooting docs and prepare a tested release
candidate. Energy claims will be made only if suitable measurement equipment is
available. Request-level receipts, hardware attestation and compliance certification are
outside this request. Code, documentation and test material produced for the effort will
be public under appropriate open licenses, preserving upstream attribution. Publish
progress against these milestones. This is one funding need: other grants or
sponsorships for overlapping work would be disclosed and the budget reduced to avoid
double funding.

The schedule assumes roughly 20 hours each week for 12 weeks.

Indra is at the Windows release-candidate stage. External adoption has not been
measured. The integration is MIT-licensed and incorporates the Hermes agent
framework from Nous Research, with original notices retained. AI tools have
assisted development, documentation and this funding application. No project
grants, prizes, sponsorships or other funding have been received.
